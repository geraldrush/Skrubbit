### The prices above are a starting point, not a quote

They are the current catalogue prices from the Skrubb It shop. Before they
go on letterhead, three things must be settled:

1. **Are they VAT inclusive?** The RFQ says prices *"must be VAT Inclusive
   and prices quoted will be deemed VAT inclusive."* If the catalogue is
   VAT exclusive and Skrubb It is VAT registered, quoting the catalogue
   number unchanged gives away 15%.
2. **Delivery to Tshaulu is not in them.** Prices must include delivery.
   Tshaulu-Tshilaphala is roughly 20 km beyond Thohoyandou.
3. **Pine gel has no size in the spec.** Every other line states one. Ask,
   or state the size you are quoting so the comparison is fair.

**Ask SAPS what they expect here.** The clause is written for agents and
sole distributors, and it demands profit margin before tax in writing. What
a manufacturer should disclose is not obvious, and guessing at a commercially
sensitive number is worse than asking.

## D. Before you send

- Confirm the **submission email address**. It is not in any of the four
  documents — it will be on the CSD RFQ detail page.
- **SBD 4 clause 2.4**: list every CSD-registered company in which any
  director holds an interest, with its MAAA number and status. Failure to
  disclose **leads to disqualification**, and CSD makes it trivial to check.
- **SBD 6.1**: claim only ownership goals the CIPC record and IDs prove.
  Page 5 makes a false claim a personal risk to the directors — up to ten
  years' restriction from state business, and criminal referral.
- Send **early**. Electronic submission fails at 13:58 as easily as at 14:01.

## E. Is this worth doing?

At one unit per line the five quoted items come to roughly **R1 200**.
Twenty-one mandatory checklist items, a health authority certificate and a
tax PIN, for an order worth about what a single 25L drum sells for.

So do it for the position, not the money: it puts Skrubb It on record as a
supplier to that station, at manufacturer margin, on items it makes anyway,
about 20 km from the Thohoyandou training site BePlugged is separately
planning. If SAPS confirms they want **unit rates to order against** rather
than a one-off, the value changes completely — that is the question worth
asking first.

If BD 6 cannot be certified inside eight days, the answer is to skip this
one and be ready for the next. A quotation that arrives invalid costs the
same effort as one that wins.
